# Song Log

*Every song written with this skill, as a fingerprint of the moves it used*

Read this before writing (step 2 of the workflow). Each song here has used up its moves. The next song keeps the techniques (a scene, a double-meaning hook, a punchline, a callback, a flip at the end) but carries them out differently on every row of the fingerprint.

After delivering a song, add its fingerprint at the bottom, newest last.

## Fingerprint rows

| Row | What to note |
|---|---|
| Scene | place, time, weather or element, what the two people are doing |
| Hook | the hook word or phrase, what kind of double meaning it has, how the hook is shaped |
| Rhymes | the vowel chain in the hook and in each verse |
| Openings | how the intro and verse 1 open |
| Dialogue | who speaks, and the first word of the quote |
| Punchlines | the joke in each verse and how it works |
| Callback | the object planted in verse 1 and how verse 2 pays it off |
| Island detail | the place, brand or PR problem |
| Sex | the explicit images and positions |
| Outro | how the ending flips the hook |
| Structure | the section order and line counts |
| Signature phrases | phrases of 3 or more words that can't come back |

## 1. Humedá (cloud trap, 2:30, approved)

| Row | Used |
|---|---|
| Scene | August in Santurce, AC broken, 90% humidity, then an aguacero on the zinc roof. Indoors, in bed. |
| Hook | *Humedá*: the humidity and her wetness. One word, said the PR way, echoed in (parentheses). |
| Rhymes | hook on -á (mojá, sudá, pegá, ma); verses on -ao (dañao, entrao, amarrao, sudao) |
| Openings | intro whispered *"Mmm... noventa por ciento (ey) / Humedá..."*; verse 1 opens with month + barrio |
| Dialogue | she speaks: *"Acho, qué calor"* |
| Punchlines | *pues quítatelo* (she complains about the heat, so take it off); the coquí goes quiet to listen |
| Callback | his t-shirt on her in verse 1, soaked on the floor in verse 2 |
| Island detail | *el aire* = the AC, aguacero, zinc roof, coquí |
| Sex | her on top, *dando vuelta'*, *chingándote al ritmo del abanico* |
| Outro | *Ya escampó allá afuera... aquí adentro, no*: outside it stopped, inside it didn't |
| Structure | Intro 2 · V1 6 · Pre 2 · Coro 8 · V2 6 · Coro 8 · Outro 3 |
| Signature phrases | *¿qué tú 'tás esperando?* · *y tú pidiendo má'* · *que se joda el aire* · *no pase ni el aire* · *dando vuelta'* |

## 2. Brilla (cloud trap, 2:30, liked but "just like the other song")

| Row | Used |
|---|---|
| Scene | moonless Tuesday night, the bioluminescent bay at La Parguera, in the water |
| Hook | *Brilla*: the water glowing and her glowing. One word, echoed in (parentheses). |
| Rhymes | hook on i-a (brilla, orilla, prendía, encima); verse 1 on e-o; verse 2 on -ao |
| Openings | intro whispered *"Mmm... sin luna (ey) / Brilla..."*; verse 1 opens with day + place |
| Dialogue | she speaks: *"Acho, esto no se ve"* |
| Punchlines | *y te quita' el resto* (nobody can see, so she strips); *alumbramo' má' que LUMA* |
| Callback | her phone story comes out black in verse 1; in verse 2 that's better, because it's private |
| Island detail | La Parguera, LUMA |
| Sex | her on top, *bájate lento, quédate encima*, legs around his waist, *te lo meto lento* |
| Outro | *Ya salimo' del agua... y tú todavía brilla*: same "it ended out there, not in here" flip |
| Structure | identical to Humedá |
| Signature phrases | *el agua se entera* · *ni luna hace falta* · *tú 'tás prendía* |

### Why it read as a copy

Its scene and hook word were new, but almost everything else was the same as "Humedá": a wet, sensual setting where the element is the hook, the same structure and line counts, the same whispered *"Mmm... (ey)"* intro, a one-word hook with the same echo, the -ao rhymes in verse 2, a quote that opens with *"Acho"* and leads to taking clothes off, her on top, the same "outside it stopped, she didn't" outro, and a near-reuse of *y tú pidiendo má'* / *esto no se ha acabao*.
