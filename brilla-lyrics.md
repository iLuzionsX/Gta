## The story

A moonless Tuesday night in the bioluminescent bay at La Parguera. They cut the boat's motor, she tries to film a story and the video comes out black, so she figures nobody can see and takes off the rest. In the water, every move they make glows blue. *Brilla* is the water glowing and her glowing, and the water "finds out" what they're doing because it lights up.

## How it's built

| Line | What makes it boricua / what it does |
|---|---|
| *Martes sin luna, La Parguera en silencio* | Time + real place in line one. La Parguera (Lajas) is a bio bay where you can still get in the water; "sin luna" is a real detail (it only glows well on dark nights), not a poetic moon. |
| *Tú grabando un story y sale to' negro* | Digital-life detail everyone who's been there knows: the glow never shows up on a phone camera. Plants the callback. |
| *"Acho, esto no se ve"... y te quita' el resto* | Verse 1 punchline: she means the video, then uses it as the excuse to strip. Dialogue opened with *acho*. |
| *Me grita': "¿Y tú te va' a quedar seco?"* | She's the one daring him, so the desire is mutual. Verse 1 rhymes on e-o: silencio, medio, negro, resto, cuerpo, seco. |
| *Pero el agua se entera* | Pre-coro twist: nobody sees them, but the water "finds out" because it lights up wherever they move. |
| *Brilla / Tú te mueve' y el agua brilla* | Title = hook. Double meaning: the bioluminescence, and her. Hook chain on i-a: brilla, orilla, prendía, encima. |
| *Ni luna hace falta, tú 'tás prendía* | Hook punchline. *Prendía* = lit up, turned on, and high. |
| *Que cada vez que tú baja'... brilla* | The repeating cell, with an explicit image that stays sensual; the hook ends on the word it opened with. |
| *Alumbramo' má' que LUMA en to' el año* | Verse 2 punchline and the 2020s island jab: two people in a bay light up more than the grid operator does all year. |
| *El story salió negro... mejor, que es privao* | The verse 1 callback pays off: the black video means the night stays theirs. Verse 2 rhymes on a-o: salao, alumbrao, pedazo, año, privao, acabao. |
| *Ya salimo' del agua... y tú todavía brilla* | The outro flips the hook: out of the water, and she's still glowing. |

## Suno tips

- Paste the style prompt into **Style** and only the code block into **Lyrics**.
- If the falsetto in the coro comes out too strong, change the tag to `[Coro – falsete suave, breathy]`.
- If it says "LUMA" like a word it doesn't know, respell it *Lúma*.
- If verse 2 feels rushed, cut *"Y esto no se ha acabao (no)"* first.
